About TrueNorth Group of Companies

TrueNorth Group of Companies Ltd. is a Liberian-owned holding company. We own and oversee three operating subsidiaries working in sanitation, drinking water, and logistics across Liberia, and we keep strategy at the holding-company level so delivery can stay focused at the subsidiary level.

Governance first, services second

Government agencies, NGOs, and development partners work with us because we operate the way an institutional partner needs us to: documented, auditable, and consistent. We run TrueNorth as a governance structure first and a services company second — see our full governance and accountability framework for how that's structured.

It's also why our subsidiaries can move quickly on the ground. The standard is written down, reviewed regularly, and applied the same way whether the contract is with a government agency or a single household.

Operating principles

01

Integrity

The same standard applies whether we're signing a government contract or picking up a resident's trash bin. No separate rules for who's watching.

02

Service

We measure ourselves on whether the water arrived, the route ran, and the delivery landed, not on how the pitch deck reads.

03

Accountability to our clients

If something goes wrong, we say so, document it, and fix it. Our reporting is built to be checked, not just read.
