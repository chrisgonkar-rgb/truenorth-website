Drinking-Water Production & Distribution in Liberia

TrueNorth Group produces and distributes drinking water across Liberia through its subsidiary, Oasis Pure Water Services Ltd. Quality control is checked at each stage of production, not only at the end of the line.

Who we serve

Oasis Pure supplies institutional and residential customers across Liberia, including businesses, offices, and organizations that need a documented, consistent water supplier rather than an informal one.

What's included

Production quality control

Checks built into each stage of production, not only the finished bottle.

Bulk & retail distribution

Supply for institutional accounts as well as retail-scale distribution.

Consistent supply

Structured delivery for organizations that need to plan around a reliable water supplier.

This service is delivered by Oasis Pure Water Services Ltd. under TrueNorth Group's governance framework. Institutional partners evaluating Oasis Pure as a vendor can find documentation requirements on our procurement page.
